# Website Manager & Developer (AI-Enabled)
Company: not named (a group of companies) · Location: not stated · Pay: AED 8,000 - 9,000 per month · Source: pasted by user (no URL)

We're looking for a Website Manager & Developer to both build and maintain the digital presence across our group of companies. This dual role covers two core areas: developing new websites and platforms tailored to each company's specific needs, and managing and maintaining existing sites to keep them secure, current, and high-performing. We expect this person to actively adopt and leverage AI tools to work faster and smarter — using AI to accelerate development, streamline maintenance, and add value across the portfolio. You'll take projects from concept to launch, own them long-term, and continually bring modern, AI-driven efficiency to how the group's sites are built and run.

## Key Responsibilities
### Development (building new)
- Design and develop new websites and digital platforms from concept to launch, tailored to each company's needs.
- Recommend and build the right solution for each requirement — whether a CMS site, custom web application, e-commerce store, or landing page.
- Use AI-assisted development tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Cursor, AI code assistants) to build faster and more efficiently.
- Handle both front-end and back-end development for new builds.
- Select appropriate technologies, platforms, and integrations to fit each project's goals and budget.

### Management (maintaining existing)
- Manage, maintain, and update multiple existing websites across the group, ensuring uptime, performance, and security.
- Perform ongoing updates, bug fixes, enhancements, and content changes.
- Manage hosting, domains, SSL certificates, backups, and CMS/server administration.
- Optimize existing sites for speed, SEO, mobile responsiveness, and user experience.

### AI adoption (across both)
- Actively adopt, test, and integrate AI tools to improve development speed, content creation, and site management.
- Use AI for tasks such as code generation, debugging, content drafting, image generation/editing, SEO, and workflow automation.
- Stay current with emerging AI tools and recommend ones that benefit the group's websites and processes.
- Apply sound judgment on where AI adds value and where human oversight is essential — particularly around quality, security, and licensing.

### Across all areas
- Coordinate with marketing, design, and management teams across different companies to gather requirements and deliver solutions.
- Maintain brand consistency across each site while respecting each company's distinct identity.
- Implement and monitor analytics, tracking, and reporting (Google Analytics, Search Console, etc.).
- Ensure compliance with data protection, accessibility, and security best practices.
- Troubleshoot issues and provide ongoing technical support across all group sites.
- Manage third-party vendors, plugins, and integrations where needed.
